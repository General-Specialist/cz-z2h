dot product of a and b = |a| |b| cos(theta)
perpendicular if dot product is 0
cross product of a and b = |a| |b| sin(theta)
parallel if cross product is 0
unit vectors have magnitude 1
area of a parallelogram: magnitude of cross product
area of a triangle: magnitude of cross product
vector valued function: a function with in real input(s) and a vector output
function of several variables: a function with a point as input and a number as output
arc length: integrate magnitude of the derivative
